The Science — Simply Explained

What is Scalar Energy?

Scalar energy is a form of electromagnetic energy described in physics as having magnitude but no directional component — it exists as a field rather than a wave moving through space. This unique property means it can penetrate physical matter in ways that conventional electromagnetic waves cannot.

Why it matters

Scalar waves interact with living systems in ways conventional technology cannot.

Our cells naturally produce and respond to electromagnetic signals. Research in the field of biophysics — the study of the physics of living things — shows that these cellular signals help regulate everything from cell growth to how our immune system functions.

When scalar waves interact with biological tissue, they appear to support the body’s own regulatory systems — helping cells communicate more effectively, reducing the effects of environmental electromagnetic stress (from phones, WiFi etc.), and potentially supporting the body’s natural self-healing processes.

It is worth noting that scalar energy research is still an emerging field. While many practitioners and users report impressive results, it sits at the frontier of science — which is exactly why it is so exciting, and why we encourage everyone to experience it for themselves.

The History of Scalar Healing Technology
1880s

Nikola Tesla — The Discovery of Scalar Waves

Serbian-American inventor Nikola Tesla first described and demonstrated what he called “non-Hertzian” waves — energy that could be transmitted through space without conventional electromagnetic propagation. Tesla believed this energy could be used to transmit power wirelessly across the globe, and conducted many experiments exploring its properties. His Wardenclyffe Tower project was designed to harness this very energy. While many of his ideas were suppressed or dismissed in his lifetime, modern quantum physics has confirmed the existence of scalar-type fields, vindicating much of his pioneering work.

1930s

Royal Raymond Rife — Frequency Healing

Dr Royal Raymond Rife, an American inventor and microscopist, developed a powerful microscope capable of viewing living viruses — something previously thought impossible. His research led him to discover that every micro-organism has its own specific “resonant frequency” — and that by broadcasting that frequency back at it, the organism could be neutralised. His device, the Rife machine, became one of the earliest examples of frequency-based healthcare technology. Despite remarkable reported results, Rife’s work faced significant opposition from the established medical community of the time. Today, frequency medicine is experiencing a remarkable renaissance.

1970s–90s

Biophysics — Science Catches Up

Throughout the latter half of the 20th century, research into the bioelectromagnetic properties of living systems advanced significantly. Scientists including Fritz-Albert Popp (who discovered that cells communicate via “biophotons” — light emitted by living cells) helped establish that the body’s energy field is a real, measurable phenomenon. This body of research provided the scientific framework upon which modern quantum healing technologies are built.
