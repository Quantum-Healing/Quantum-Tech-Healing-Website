About the RASHA Machine

The RASHA (Resonance & Atomic Scalar Harmonics Assembly) is a quantum wellness device that generates scalar electromagnetic waves — a type of energy first explored by Nikola Tesla over a century ago. It is designed to interact with the body’s natural energy field, helping to restore balance and coherence at a cellular and energetic level.

Think of it like a tuning fork for your entire energy system. It doesn’t do anything to your body directly — it creates the energetic conditions in which your body can do its own healing work more effectively.

You can read a much more detailed explanation on our RASHA page.

No. The RASHA machine is a wellness device, not a medical device. It is not registered or approved for the diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of any medical condition. It is designed to support general wellbeing through quantum scalar energy technology.

If you have a medical condition, please continue working with your GP or specialist. The RASHA is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical care. See our Legislation page for full details.

The RASHA uses scalar electromagnetic waves — a specific type of energy that operates differently from conventional electromagnetic waves (like those from phones or WiFi). Scalar waves are longitudinal rather than transverse, which means they interact with living systems in a fundamentally different way.

Unlike RIFE machines (which broadcast frequencies externally), the RASHA both scans your bioenergetic field and responds to it — creating a more personalised, dynamic interaction. And unlike Reiki or similar hands-on energy work, the RASHA uses specific measurable frequencies rather than practitioner-directed intention alone.

The RASHA is a relatively new technology, and large-scale clinical trials specific to it are not yet available. However, the principles it is based on — scalar wave physics, bioenergetics, biophoton coherence, and quantum biology — are well-documented areas of scientific research.

The scientists whose work underpins these principles include Nobel laureates and respected academics across the fields of physics and biology. You can read more about this on our Science page.

What we do have is extensive anecdotal evidence from users worldwide, consistent patterns in reported experiences, and an ever-growing body of practitioner observation. We believe the most reliable form of evidence is your own experience — which is why we encourage an open-minded, exploratory approach.

About Sessions & Treatments

You will sit or lie comfortably near the RASHA device. The session is entirely non-invasive — nothing touches your body, and you remain fully clothed throughout. The operator will run the session, adjusting settings gently as needed.

Most sessions last between 45 and 90 minutes depending on the treatment chosen. Many people enter a deeply relaxed, meditative state. Some drift into light sleep. Others remain alert throughout — all experiences are normal and valid.

See our Treatments page for a full guide on what to expect before, during and after your session.

Experiences vary widely from person to person. Some people notice physical sensations such as warmth, tingling, or a feeling of lightness or floating. Many report a profound sense of deep calm. Some feel nothing particular during the session itself, but notice significant effects in the hours and days afterwards.

There is no “correct” experience. The RASHA works at a level beyond conscious perception — what matters is what shifts in your body and energy field over time, not necessarily what you feel in the moment.

This depends entirely on the individual and what they are hoping to achieve. Many people notice meaningful shifts after a single session. For deeper or more longstanding imbalances, a series of sessions spread across a few weeks often produces more lasting results.

We generally recommend an initial introductory session, followed by a discussion with the operator about the best path forward. Some clients find monthly maintenance sessions sufficient; others prefer more intensive periods of sessions at the start.

We never pressure anyone into more sessions than feels right for them. This is your journey, at your pace.

Drink plenty of water in the 24 hours after your session — this supports your body in processing and integrating the energetic work done during the session. Rest if you feel the need. Avoid heavy alcohol consumption on the day of your session.

Pay attention to your body in the days following — note any changes in sleep, energy, mood, or how you feel physically. These observations are valuable and worth sharing with the operator at your next session.

Some people experience a mild “detox-like” response in the first 24 hours — this can include temporary fatigue, vivid dreams, or heightened emotions. This is normal and typically passes quickly.
