Legal & Regulatory

Legislation & Policies

This page sets out the legal framework within which Quantum Tech Healing Centre operates, including important disclaimers, our privacy policy, and the regulatory context for complementary wellness services in the United Kingdom.

! Important: Please read all sections of this page carefully before using our services. By booking a session with us you confirm that you have read and understood the information on this page.

1. Medical Disclaimer

The Quantum Tech Healing Centre provides wellness services using the RASHA quantum scalar energy machine. The RASHA machine is not a medical device and has not been registered or approved by the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) or any other regulatory authority for the purpose of diagnosing, treating, curing, or preventing any disease or medical condition.

Nothing on this website, nor anything said or provided during a session at the Quantum Tech Healing Centre, constitutes or should be taken as medical advice. The information and services provided are for general wellness and educational purposes only.

You should always seek the advice of a qualified medical professional regarding any medical condition, symptom, or health concern you may have. Do not disregard, delay, or alter any medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment on the basis of information provided by this website or through our services.

Our services are intended to complement, not to replace, conventional medical care. We strongly encourage all clients to maintain their relationship with their GP and any relevant medical specialists.

2. Complementary & Alternative Therapy Disclaimer

Quantum Tech Healing Centre operates as a complementary wellness service. Complementary and alternative therapies are not regulated in the same way as conventional medical treatments in the United Kingdom. Practitioners of complementary therapies are not required to hold medical qualifications unless they are also registered healthcare professionals.

The RASHA machine and related quantum wellness approaches fall within the category of complementary and alternative wellness practices. While many users report positive subjective experiences and improvements in their wellbeing, individual results vary considerably and cannot be guaranteed.

No client should book a session with the intention of replacing prescribed medical treatment, nor should any session be presented as a substitute for emergency medical care. In the event of a medical emergency, please contact the emergency services immediately (UK: 999).

3. Use of Testimonials

Testimonials published on this website represent the individual, subjective experiences of the clients who provided them. They are published with the full consent of the individuals concerned, or provided anonymously at their request.

Testimonials are not intended to constitute, and should not be interpreted as, claims of medical efficacy or therapeutic benefit. They reflect personal experiences which may not be representative of the results experienced by other individuals.

In compliance with UK advertising standards (ASA/CAP Code), Quantum Tech Healing Centre does not make medical claims through the use of testimonials or any other content published on this website.

4. Advertising Standards Compliance

Quantum Tech Healing Centre is committed to compliance with the standards set by the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) and the Committee of Advertising Practice (CAP Code) in the United Kingdom.

We do not make claims that our services can diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any named medical condition. All descriptions of potential benefits are framed in terms of general wellbeing, subjective experience, and the principles of quantum field science — and are not intended as medical claims.

If you believe any content on our website contravenes advertising standards, please contact us so we may address your concern.

5. Consumer Rights (UK)

As a UK-based consumer, your statutory rights are not affected by anything on this page. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, you are entitled to services that are:

  • Performed with reasonable care and skill
  • Provided at a reasonable price (where no price has been agreed in advance)
  • Carried out within a reasonable time

Our cancellation policy and booking terms will be provided to you at the point of booking. If you have a complaint about the service you have received, please contact us in the first instance so we can seek to resolve it directly.

6. Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

Quantum Tech Healing Centre takes your privacy seriously. This section sets out how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What data we collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, and location — provided via contact or questionnaire forms
  • Health and wellbeing information — provided in the pre-session questionnaire for the purpose of tailoring your session
  • Session notes — brief notes made by the operator for continuity of care
  • Testimonials — where provided with explicit consent for publication

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiries and manage your bookings
  • To tailor your RASHA sessions to your individual needs
  • To maintain session records for continuity of care
  • To publish testimonials where explicit consent has been given

We do not sell, share, or otherwise disclose your personal data to third parties without your explicit consent, except where required by law.

Data retention

Client records are retained for a maximum of [X] years from the date of your last session, after which they are securely deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your rights under UK GDPR

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restrict processing: You may request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: You may request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Right to object: You may object to certain types of data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are not satisfied with how we handle your data.

Cookies

This website currently does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Health & Safety at Sessions

Quantum Tech Healing Centre is committed to the health and safety of all clients and staff. Sessions are conducted in a clean, safe, and comfortable environment. The RASHA machine is operated in accordance with the manufacturer’s guidelines.

We require all clients to disclose relevant health information prior to their first session through our questionnaire. Certain conditions — including pregnancy, presence of a pacemaker or implanted electronic device, and active cancer treatment — may contraindicate the use of the RASHA machine. If you have any concerns about your suitability, please contact us before booking.

The operator reserves the right to decline or pause a session if they have reason to believe it may not be in the best interests of the client’s safety or wellbeing.
