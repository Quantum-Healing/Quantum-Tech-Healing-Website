What clients experience

Transformation does not announce itself. It arrives quietly — in the way you sleep, the way you respond, the life you begin to choose.

In session, many clients notice deep calm, altered states of awareness, emotional release, or unexpected mental clarity. These are not side effects — they are the system beginning to clear.

In the days and weeks that follow, clients typically report behavioural shifts that arrive without force: increased confidence, clearer direction, a growing alignment between their inner state and outer life. Over time this becomes something deeper — identity-level change, a different relationship with the self, a life moving in a direction that finally feels true.

More than healing

This work goes beyond traditional energy healing. It is closer to what some would call true deliverance.

Energetic blockages do not disappear on their own. Subconscious patterns that were formed under stress, fear, or trauma do not dissolve through willpower. They require a different kind of intervention — one that speaks the language the body and mind actually respond to: frequency, resonance, and field.

What the RASHA facilitates is the activation of a higher state of awareness within the system — one in which the body recognises what it has been carrying, releases it, and recalibrates toward its natural internal frequency. Not managed. Not suppressed. Cleared.

Who this is for

Not everyone is ready for this level of work. Those who are tend to know it.

High performers who have reached a ceiling they cannot explain. People who have done the therapy, read the books, tried the retreats — and still feel something unresolved at a deeper level. Individuals who are done with managing and ready to actually change.

Also those seeking alignment between their energy, their ambition, and their purpose — people who understand that abundance is not purely a financial state, but an internal one. When the frequency is right, the life follows.

Before & After

What to Expect

Before your session

  • →

    Complete our online questionnaire before your first visit. This helps us tailor your session to your specific needs.

  • →

    Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. You will remain fully clothed throughout the session.

  • →

    Drink plenty of water in the 24 hours before your appointment. Hydration supports the body’s energy conductivity.

  • →

    Avoid heavy meals in the two hours before your session. A light snack is fine.

  • →

    Come with an open mind and set a clear intention for what you would like from your session.

During your session

  • →

    You will sit or lie comfortably near the RASHA device. The session is completely non-invasive — nothing touches your body.

  • →

    Many people find sessions deeply relaxing — some drift into a meditative state or light sleep. This is entirely normal and welcome.

  • →

    You may notice sensations such as warmth, tingling, or a gentle sense of “lightness.” Some notice nothing at all during the session — both are completely normal.

  • →

    Our operator will be present throughout to monitor the session and adjust settings as needed.

After your session

  • →

    Drink plenty of water for the rest of the day. This helps your body process and integrate the energetic shifts from the session.

  • →

    Rest if you feel the need to. Some people feel energised; others feel ready for a deep, restorative sleep. Follow your body’s guidance.

  • →

    Some people notice a short “detox-like” response in the first 24 hours — mild fatigue or heightened emotions. This is a natural sign that the body is processing. It passes quickly.

  • →

    Benefits often deepen over the days following a session, and are generally cumulative over a series of treatments.

Is it suitable for me?

RASHA sessions are generally suitable for most adults. However, we ask that you do not attend if you are pregnant, have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, or are currently undergoing active cancer treatment. If you have any other medical conditions, please consult your GP before booking, and do let us know when completing your questionnaire.

If in doubt, please contact us — we are always happy to discuss your individual circumstances before you book.
